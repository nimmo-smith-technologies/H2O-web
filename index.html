    3. Plot

    ResultCensored result✕Outlier (excluded)Trend
    • Censored results are ones the laboratory could only report as below (<) or above (>) a limit. For example “<0.5” means “less than 0.5”, usually because the amount was too small to measure precisely. They are drawn as hollow circles at the limit value. They are included in the line, mean, minimum, maximum and trend, taken at that limit value, so the trend can be biased when many results are censored.
    • Trend is a straight line fitted by least squares through the plotted results (after removing outliers if ticked). It is a rough guide only: there is no significance test and no allowance for seasons.
    • Outliers are results more than 1.5 × the interquartile range below the lower quartile or above the upper quartile (Tukey’s rule). Water data are often skewed, so a flagged value is unusual, not necessarily an error. This needs at least 8 results.
